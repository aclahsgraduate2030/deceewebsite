THE SHUTDOWN OF NIGHTNET

Nightnet was such a good-running platform. Been here since the beginning of time (almost 150 years!), but all things come to an end. This may not be what the users wanted, but it is what we had to do, since the king ordered us to make a new platform for everybody, and stop the development of this app. Meaning that all of this stuff will be an archive. All profiles will be archived including the posts, and nobody can use the app anymore. I hope you understand it was not our intention to do this, we intend to make a better website for all. Love and suppor from Nightnet.
